About

Hi, I'm Mae

Graphic and web designer with over ten years of experience across branding, print, digital and presentation design, creating clean, polished work that communicates clearly.

I believe good design starts with clarity. Every layout, color and type choice should serve the message and make it easier for people to understand and act on. I approach each project with fresh eyes, taking the time to understand my clients' goals so the final result reflects their vision.

Outside of design, I enjoy the outdoors, music and reading. A good hike or a quiet walk is usually where my next idea shows up.
